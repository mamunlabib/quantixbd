Full Description

The Anker Zolo 20W IQ & PD Type-C Charger is your perfect everyday fast charging companion. Designed to be compact and portable, this charger delivers 20W of Power Delivery output — enough to fast charge your iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, and most modern USB-C devices quickly and safely.

Powered by Anker's exclusive IQ technology, the charger intelligently detects your device and delivers the optimal charging power — so your device charges faster without risk of overheating or overloading. The MultiProtect safety system adds another layer of protection with temperature control, overvoltage protection, and short-circuit prevention built in.
